# 🌱 GreenPulse AI

### AI-Powered Energy Analytics & Sustainability Assistant

GreenPulse AI is an intelligent energy analytics platform that helps users understand, monitor, and optimize energy consumption.

Users can upload energy consumption data in CSV format and receive analytical insights, appliance-level consumption breakdowns, anomaly detection, carbon-footprint estimates, energy forecasts, AI-powered recommendations, and interactive what-if simulations.

The platform combines **data analytics, machine learning, forecasting, and Generative AI** to turn raw energy data into actionable sustainability insights.

---

## Features

### Energy Consumption Analysis

Upload a CSV file and automatically calculate:

- Total energy consumption
- Average energy consumption
- Highest consumption event
- Appliance-wise energy usage
- Energy efficiency score
- Consumption time-series data

---

### Anomaly Detection

GreenPulse identifies unusual energy consumption patterns using statistical analysis.

The system calculates **Z-scores** for energy readings and flags readings that exceed the configured anomaly threshold.

This helps identify potentially abnormal consumption events.

---

### Sustainability & Carbon Footprint

The platform converts energy consumption into sustainability-oriented metrics, including:

- Estimated CO₂ emissions
- Equivalent driving distance
- Estimated trees required to offset emissions

These metrics make energy consumption easier to understand from an environmental perspective.

---

###  Energy Forecasting

GreenPulse predicts future energy consumption using historical time-series data.

The forecasting pipeline supports:

- Time-series preprocessing
- Forecast generation
- Future consumption visualization
- Forecast interpretation

The resulting forecast is displayed directly on the dashboard.

---

### AI-Powered Energy Insights

Google Gemini analyzes the generated energy statistics and provides:

- Overall consumption summary
- Key findings
- Personalized recommendations
- Potential reasons for high consumption
- Environmental observations
- Priority actions

The AI analysis is grounded in the supplied energy data rather than relying on hardcoded recommendations.

---

###  AI Energy Assistant

GreenPulse includes an AI chat assistant that allows users to ask questions about their uploaded energy data.

Example questions:

Which appliance consumes the most energy?

Why is my energy consumption high?

What should I reduce first?

What does my efficiency score mean?
